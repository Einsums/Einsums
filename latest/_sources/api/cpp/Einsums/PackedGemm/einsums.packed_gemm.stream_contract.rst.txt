=====================================
einsums::packed_gemm::stream_contract
=====================================

.. note::
   Generated from the C++ headers by ``apiary --emit-cpp-docs-json``.

Defined in header ``Einsums/PackedGemm/Stream.hpp``.

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::packed_gemm

.. cpp:function:: template <typename T> void stream_contract(const T *s, const StreamLayout &s_layout, const std::vector<StreamTerm<T>> &terms, const std::vector<int> &partition_axes)

   Evaluate every term with one read of :math:`S`, in storage order.

   A contraction in which one operand supplies every index and the other operands are small is
   memory-bandwidth bound: its cost is one read of :math:`S`. This walks :math:`S` once, fastest axis
   innermost, and adds each element's contribution to every term's output, so :math:`N` terms over one
   tensor cost one read of it instead of :math:`N`.

   The team is sized to the work: a stream too short to pay for forking threads runs serially and
   writes every output in place. A parallel walk accumulates outputs in per-thread private buffers and
   reduces them at the end, unless ``partition_axes`` names axes of :math:`S` the walk may split between
   threads instead: the kernel partitions one of them (preferring an axis with a block for every
   thread, then the largest stride), and each term whose output carries that axis writes its own
   disjoint slice directly. Terms sharing an output must put the axis in the same place in it: one on
   the rows and another on the columns would hand each thread crossing stripes, so such a term is
   privatized instead. Every term whose output does not carry the chosen axis is privatized too, so
   the caller must keep those outputs small enough for a copy per thread to stay in cache.

   Each output's ``c_pf`` is applied once, before any accumulation, even when :math:`S` is empty. The work
   is split over the threads the parallel region actually receives, so a call from inside another
   parallel region, where the region does not fork, runs correctly on one thread.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :param s: The streamed tensor's data.
   :param s_layout: Its geometry.
   :param terms: The contractions to evaluate. No output may overlap :math:`S`, a small operand, or another output.
   :param partition_axes: Axes of :math:`S` the walk may partition for direct writes; empty to privatize every output.
   :throws std::invalid_argument: If a term's axis maps do not match :math:`S`'s rank, or a term after the first on an output has a ``c_pf`` other than 1.
