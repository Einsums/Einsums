.. cpp:class:: ContractionSpec

   Describes the topology of a tensor contraction.

   Indices may repeat (e.g., a_indices = {"i","i"} for a Hadamard A[i,i]).
   all_indices is the unique loop space: target_indices ++ link_indices.
