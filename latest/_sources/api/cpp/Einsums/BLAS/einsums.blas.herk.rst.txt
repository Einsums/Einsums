einsums::blas::herk

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::blas

.. cpp:function:: template <typename T> void herk(char uplo, char trans, int_t n, int_t k, RemoveComplexT<T> alpha, const T *a, int_t lda, RemoveComplexT<T> beta, T *c, int_t ldc)

   Hermitian rank-k update.

   Computes :math:`\mathbf{C} := \alpha \mathbf{A}\mathbf{A}^H + \beta \mathbf{C}`
   (trans='N') or :math:`\mathbf{C} := \alpha \mathbf{A}^H\mathbf{A} + \beta \mathbf{C}`
   (trans='C'). :math:`\mathbf{C}` is Hermitian, so ``alpha`` and ``beta`` are
   real and only the referenced triangle is updated.

   :param uplo: Which triangle of C is referenced and updated ('U' or 'L').
   :param trans: 'N': update with A*A^H; 'C': with A^H*A.
   :param n: Order of C.
   :param k: The other dimension of A (see ``trans``).
   :param alpha: Real scalar on the product.
   :param a: The matrix A.
   :param lda: Leading dimension of a.
   :param beta: Real scalar on C.
   :param c: The Hermitian matrix C, updated in place.
   :param ldc: Leading dimension of c.
