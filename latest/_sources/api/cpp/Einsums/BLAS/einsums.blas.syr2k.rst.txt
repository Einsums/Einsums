einsums::blas::syr2k

.. cpp:function:: template <typename T> void syr2k(char uplo, char trans, int_t n, int_t k, T alpha, const T *a, int_t lda, const T *b, int_t ldb, T beta, T *c, int_t ldc)

   Symmetric rank-2k update.

   Computes
   :math:`\mathbf{C} := \alpha(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}^T + \mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}^T) + \beta \mathbf{C}`
   (trans='N') or
   :math:`\mathbf{C} := \alpha(\mathbf{A}^T\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{A}) + \beta \mathbf{C}`
   (trans='T'), updating only the referenced triangle of the symmetric result.

   :param uplo: Which triangle of C is referenced and updated ('U' or 'L').
   :param trans: Selects the update form as above.
   :param n: Order of C.
   :param k: The other dimension of A and B.
   :param alpha: Scalar on the products.
   :param a: The matrix A.
   :param lda: Leading dimension of a.
   :param b: The matrix B.
   :param ldb: Leading dimension of b.
   :param beta: Scalar on C.
   :param c: The symmetric matrix C, updated in place.
   :param ldc: Leading dimension of c.
