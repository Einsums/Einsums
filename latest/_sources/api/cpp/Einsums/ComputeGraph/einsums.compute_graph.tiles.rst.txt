.. cpp:function:: SpaceTiling tiles(std::vector<int> sizes)

   A tiled axis with a partition and no space.

   The tiled counterpart of ``fixed``: an axis that is cut up but means nothing chemically,
   so it gets no space and no dim symbol and a bind may not move it.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :param sizes: The tile sizes.
   :returns: The axis.
