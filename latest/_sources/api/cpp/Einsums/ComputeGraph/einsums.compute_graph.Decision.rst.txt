.. cpp:class:: Decision

   One thing a pass did, declined or said, as a record.

   What ``OptimizerPass::explain`` and ``OptimizerPass::skip_reasons`` print is rendered from
   these; a program reads the fields instead. A record from a call a pass has not yet given a
   ``Reason`` has an empty ``code`` and carries its words in ``reason`` and ``text``.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

      :returns: Its value, or 0 when the decision carries no such fact.
