.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::compute_graph

.. cpp:function:: SymbolicCost symbolic_cost_for(const EinsumDescriptor &desc)

   The symbolic cost of one einsum node, derived mechanically from its contraction spec.

   - ``SymbolicCost::flops`` is ``2`` times the product of the distinct letters of the loop space (``ContractionSpec::all_indices``), the two counting the multiply and the add. A letter repeated within an operand appears once, matching the loop nest rather than the index list.
   - ``SymbolicCost::traffic`` is the sum of the three operand sizes, C first, then A and B, each from ``symbolic_size_for``. Every operand is counted once, which is what a streaming kernel costs and what a blocked one approaches.
   - ``SymbolicCost::resident`` equals the traffic polynomial. See ``SymbolicCost`` for why that approximation is used and what it costs.

   Letters absent from ``EinsumDescriptor::letter_spaces`` get anonymous variables, so an
   unannotated node still yields a comparable cost, just one the scale-order rung cannot rank.

   :param desc: The node's descriptor.
   :returns: Its flops, traffic and resident polynomials.
