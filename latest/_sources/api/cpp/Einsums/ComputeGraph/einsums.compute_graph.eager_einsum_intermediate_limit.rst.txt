einsums::compute_graph::eager_einsum_intermediate_limit
=======================================================

.. note::

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::compute_graph

.. cpp:function:: std::size_t eager_einsum_intermediate_limit(std::size_t element_size)

   The most elements an eager einsum over several operands lets a partial product hold.

   A captured einsum is not limited: its partial products are graph intermediates, and the graph's
   ``AxisTiling`` pass builds one a block at a time when it is too large to hold. An eager einsum
   has no such pass, so its partial products are held to what ``--einsums:max-memory`` allows.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :param element_size: The bytes of one element.
   :returns: The limit, or zero for none.
