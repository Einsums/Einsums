einsums::compute_graph::ScaleDescriptor

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::compute_graph

.. cpp:class:: ScaleDescriptor

   Metadata for ``OpKind::Scale`` nodes: ``A *= factor``.

   Used by ScaleAbsorption to detect scales a following overwrite makes dead,
   and by ElementWiseFusion to merge consecutive scales of one tensor.

   ``factor`` is a ``PrefactorScalar``, not a ``double``, so a complex scale
   records exactly what the executor applies (CSE and ScaleAbsorption compare
   and fold it).

   ``params`` is null on nodes a pass built by hand rather than through
   ``build_executor``; readers must gate on it.

      The descriptor's name: its identity inside an ``OpData`` and in a saved graph.

      The scaling factor (at-capture snapshot).
