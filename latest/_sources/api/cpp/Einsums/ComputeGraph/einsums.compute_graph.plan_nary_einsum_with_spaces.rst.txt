einsums::compute_graph::plan_nary_einsum_with_spaces
====================================================

.. note::
   Generated from the C++ headers by ``apiary --emit-cpp-docs-json``.

Defined in header ``Einsums/ComputeGraph/NaryEinsum.hpp``.

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::compute_graph

.. cpp:function:: std::vector<NaryEinsumStep> plan_nary_einsum_with_spaces(const Graph &graph, std::string spec, std::vector<std::vector<std::size_t>> dims, std::vector<std::vector<SpaceId>> spaces, std::size_t max_intermediate_elements)

   The cheapest order to contract an einsum's operands, ranked by the index spaces they are annotated with.

   ``plan_nary_einsum`` with each letter bound to the space its axes are annotated with, by the rules a
   two-operand einsum binds its letters at capture, and ranked by the scaling ``graph's`` registry
   declares: an order that is cheaper asymptotically wins even where these extents would favour
   another, as ``MultiTermFactorization`` ranks it. A letter no annotated axis carries is
   anonymous and ranked by its extent.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :param graph: The graph whose space registry the annotations belong to.
   :param spec: The spec, three or more operands; two are planned too, as one step.
   :param dims: Each operand's extents, in the spec's order.
   :param spaces: Each operand's per-axis spaces in the spec's order, then C's, each empty where the tensor is not annotated; or empty altogether.
   :param max_intermediate_elements: As for ``plan_nary_einsum``.
   :throws std::invalid_argument: as ``plan_nary_einsum`` does, when ``spaces`` does not hold one entry per operand and one for C, or when one letter is annotated with two different spaces.
   :returns: The steps, in the order to run them.
