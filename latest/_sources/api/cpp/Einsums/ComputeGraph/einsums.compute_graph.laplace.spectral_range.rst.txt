.. cpp:function:: SpectralRange spectral_range(const std::vector<double> &axis_low, const std::vector<double> &axis_high, const std::vector<int> &signs)

   The range of :math:`\sum_k \sigma_k \varepsilon_k[i_k]` from each axis's own range.

   Exact rather than conservative, because the axes are INDEPENDENT: every combination of one
   index per axis occurs, so the extremes of the sum are the sums of the extremes.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :param axis_low: Per axis, the smallest element of that axis's energy vector.
   :param axis_high: Per axis, the largest.
   :param signs: Per axis, ``+1`` or ``-1``.
   :throws std::invalid_argument: When the three lists differ in length or are empty.
   :returns: The range of the signed sum.
