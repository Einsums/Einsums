.. cpp:function:: bool is_grouped_raisable(OpKind kind)

   Whether a GROUPED node kind can be raised into the algebra.

   Separate from ``is_raisable`` because raising one produces a statement over
   a member list rather than over a tensor, and a rewrite that reads
   ``ExprTerm::tensor`` without gating on ``ExprTerm::ragged`` would act on a
   tensor the statement does not name. A client opts in through
   ``NodeFeature::Grouped`` in ``RegionOptions::understood``; everything else keeps these as barriers.

   The two grouped kinds that are NOT here are fused kernels rather than one
   algebraic term: ``OpKind::GroupedSandwich`` dresses a slice and accumulates
   a symmetric product per auxiliary tile, and ``OpKind::GroupedGatherRotate``
   gathers a block out of a shared parent before rotating it. Neither is a
   contraction, an elementwise op or a permute, so neither has a term to raise
   to and neither has a grouped kind a rewritten term could lower back onto.

   :param kind: The op kind.
   :returns: True when a node of this kind may join a region that raises grouped nodes.
