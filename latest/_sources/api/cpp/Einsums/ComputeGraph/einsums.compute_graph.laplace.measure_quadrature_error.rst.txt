.. cpp:function:: double measure_quadrature_error(const QuadratureGrid &grid, std::size_t samples)

   The worst relative deviation of ``grid`` from :math:`1/x` over the interval it covers.

   A measurement rather than the analytic bound, and affordable precisely because the object
   being approximated is a scalar function: the four-index tensor a factorization would have to
   form to measure itself does not appear anywhere in this.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :param grid: The rule to measure.
   :param samples: How many points to sample, logarithmically spaced. At least two.
   :returns: The largest :math:`|q(x) - 1/x| \cdot |x|` found.
