==================================
einsums::compute_graph::RunMetrics
==================================

.. note::
   Generated from the C++ headers by ``apiary --emit-cpp-docs-json``.

Defined in header ``Einsums/ComputeGraph/RunMetrics.hpp``.

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::compute_graph

.. cpp:class:: RunMetrics

   What the last replay of a graph did, as ``Graph::last_run`` reports it.

   Measured, beside what the passes planned: ``peak_bytes`` is what
   ``passes::MemoryPlanning::peak_memory`` predicts, and a backing's bytes are what
   SlabStreaming's report estimates.

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0


   .. cpp:member:: double wall_ms

      Wall-clock milliseconds of the whole replay.


   .. cpp:member:: std::vector<NodeRun> nodes

      Every node the replay ran, the graph's own and its bodies'. Each graph's nodes are in the
      order they first ran, and a body's come before those of the graph holding it, since a body's
      replay finishes first.


   .. cpp:member:: std::vector<BackingRun> backings

      One entry per backing in the graph and its bodies, in name order.


   .. cpp:member:: std::uint64_t peak_bytes

      The most bytes of graph-managed tensors held at once: deferred tensors the graph allocated
      and freed, plus ``arena_bytes``. Not the process's memory: BLAS scratch is outside it, and
      the caller's own tensors are in ``resident_bytes``.


   .. cpp:member:: std::uint64_t arena_bytes

      The arena MemoryPlanning placed intermediates in, held for the whole replay; 0 without one.


   .. cpp:member:: std::uint64_t resident_bytes

      What the replay held from its start to its end outside ``peak_bytes``: the caller's
      tensors and any intermediate given storage outside a lifecycle node (@ref
      Graph::resident_bytes). The program held ``peak_bytes`` plus this at its peak.
