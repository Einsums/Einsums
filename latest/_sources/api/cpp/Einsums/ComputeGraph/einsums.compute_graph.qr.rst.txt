   QR decomposition (returning): ``A = Q * R``.

   Returns the tuple ``(Q, R)`` where ``Q`` is orthogonal (or unitary
   for complex inputs) and ``R`` is upper-triangular. Cannot be used
   during graph capture; ``A`` is left unmodified.
