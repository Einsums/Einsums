einsums::compute_graph::make_deferred_handle

.. cpp:namespace:: einsums::compute_graph

.. cpp:function:: template <GraphCapturableTensor TensorType> TensorHandle make_deferred_handle(TensorType *tensor)

   Construct the handle a DEFERRED declaration registers.

   ``make_handle`` plus the two things a declaration knows and a handle built from the tensor
   alone cannot:

   - the state is DEFERRED because the declaration says so, not because the tensor currently reads as unmaterialized. A zero-extent tensor reports itself materialized from construction, and taking that answer would leave it outside the lifecycle its declaration asked for;
   - the initialization hooks are installed unconditionally. ``make_handle`` installs them from the tensor's ``PendingInit`` tag, and a declaration sets that tag AFTER this handle exists (``declare_zero_tensor`` is ``declare_tensor`` plus a tag), so the handle a declaration registers would otherwise have no way to initialize itself.

   Everything else a deferred declaration needs - ``materialize_fn``, ``release_fn``,
   ``is_materialized_fn``, ``allreduce_sum_fn`` - is what ``make_handle`` already installs for
   any tensor type exposing the method behind it. Five declaration sites across three headers
   restated those closures on top of it.

   .. seealso::

      make_handle

   .. versionadded:: 2.0.0

   :tparam TensorType: The tensor's type.
   :param tensor: The shell tensor. Must outlive the handle.
   :returns: The handle, ready for whatever the declaring site adds to it (its ownership scope, its intermediate flag, its ``TensorHandle::init_kind``, a rank-specific resize or distribution hook).
